From AKS-117 (PR #42):

1. Input: the rules read `summary.json` and the loaded model only; every number is the summary's. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
2. Per scenario: `bottleneck`, `idle_capacity` and the first form of `not_enough_runs` are judged in each scenario and carry its id. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
3. `bottleneck`: servers only; ties by the longest mean wait, then model order; fires at or above the threshold; "busy 90% of the time it was available" for a server with a schedule (on it or its pool) or a failure. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
4. `unstable`: one finding per W201 server, `scenario` null, nothing computed. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
5. `idle_capacity`: servers only (spec 4's summary row changed to match); "no schedule" means none on the server or its pool; strictly below the threshold. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a (pools as idle capacity: Phase 2, or drop?).
6. Key metrics: objective, constraint, sink throughput and cycle time, the busiest server's wait, each once, in that order. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
7. Floors: 60 s for a time and 1 per hour for a rate, at or above; another unit judged when its mean is not zero. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
8. One finding per scenario: the widest metric's values in the text, the largest n_required (Kelton et al.). Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
9. n_required computed exactly from the decimals `summary.json` writes. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
10. The pair form: per `differences` entry, unseparated, half-width above the share of the smaller mean, `scenario` the later scenario. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
11. Measured surprise: on the Phase 1 examples the two-scenario form of `not_enough_runs` never fires, because common random numbers (spec 2) separate even scenarios 0.7% apart (each wait ± 26%, the difference 8.6 s ± 4.0 s). Its firing case is an edited copy of a run. Whether the pair form stays, is reworded, or waits for a template that decouples streams. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a (M10 templates; drop? if no template fires it).
12. `optimization_result` for a search: the wordings for improved, not improved, baseline best, infeasible baseline and infeasible search, plus the runner-up sentence; the sense from the objective the `search` names. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
13. `optimization_result` for a mathematical optimization: "Best found: {variables}. The objective is {objective}, with {tight} tight.", and the infeasible and unbounded sentences (spec 4's pattern has no `{before}` there). Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
14. Severity: `info` for `bottleneck`, `idle_capacity` and a found optimum, `warning` otherwise. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
15. Order: `optimization_result`, `unstable`, then per scenario `bottleneck`, `idle_capacity`, `not_enough_runs`, then the pairs. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
16. Display units: times in the largest unit reached, rates per hour, three figures, whole percentages in `bottleneck` and `idle_capacity`; optimization values to four figures. Candidate bucket: Phase 1a (M9 may revisit with the results screen).
17. Threshold ranges: utilizations 0 to 1, the share above 0 and at most 1, floors at least 0; and the measured values replacing spec 4's 4.8% (now 3.7%) and the prototype's 0.90 and 26% (now 0.901 and 13.3%). Candidate bucket: Phase 1a.
